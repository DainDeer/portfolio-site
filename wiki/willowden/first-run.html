01 · Summary

Value first. Understanding later.

The first run begins a real five-day cycle called settling in. It teaches Willowden through the same map, focus blocks, Rooms, and reflection that remain after onboarding is over.

first five minutes

Begin something real

Join, see the map, name—or privately choose—one thing, and start a block.

first fifteen minutes

Bank the time

Finish or stop honestly, see the map move, and receive one collection item.

first five days

Let the fit emerge

Try a few blocks, unload open loops, talk with Vel, and correct what Willowden learns.

not a complete profile not every feature not every permission not a required chat integration not a streak not a claim to already understand you
02 · Design contract

The experience has rules.

These are requirements, not aspirations. A complete implementation that makes one untrue is still the wrong implementation.

1

Action before interview

Questions about goals, friction, and capacity arrive only after useful action.

2

One invitation at a time

Show where the path leads, but make only one next move ask for attention.

3

The real product immediately

Cycle zero uses the permanent map, clock, Rooms, and event ledger.

4

Authored outside, adaptive inside

Sequence and safety are deterministic. Conversation adapts only where it helps.

5

Actions move progress

Tasks, blocks, Rooms, and honest deferrals advance the journey—not arbitrary Next buttons.

6

Nothing is failed

Invitations can be done, deferred, or declined. Silence never becomes a red mark.

7

Always on the map

Tutorial, active, upcoming, ready-to-close, and resting are all designed states.

8

Presence is controlled

Installation and warmth are never treated as permission to monitor, notify, or launch.

03 · Journey

Five days, without a streak.

Day one is the local signup date. Day five creates a reflection point, not a deadline or expiry.

day 1

Begin

Guided arrival, one real thing, and a short block. Learn only a first task and one session sample.

day 2

Return

Offer one proposed block. Open loops and a conversation with Vel are optional.

day 3

Adjust

Make the invitation smaller or better timed. Learn from correction, not compliance.

day 4

Leave room

Another low-pressure opportunity—or a quiet day that grows nothing and loses nothing.

day 5

Reflect

Show what Willowden inferred, name uncertainty, and let the person correct the draft.

A person who appears only on days one and five can still graduate. Someone who wants more time remains “ready to close,” never “day 12 of 5.”

04 · Front door

Join inside Willowden.

No chat service, browser login, or existing device is required to create the first account.

Ask only what is needed

  • An access code while signup remains gated.
  • The name Willowden should use.
  • An automatically detected timezone that can be corrected.

Pronouns, goals, working style, and personal context are not form fields. Vel can ask open questions later, and every question can be declined.

Make failure honest

Signup requires connectivity. If the service cannot be reached, Willowden preserves entered fields locally and stays on this screen. An idempotency key makes a retry return the same account after a lost response.

Approve other devices here

An authenticated device creates a short-lived link code from Settings. Linking is a product feature, not a favor requested from a cast member.

Done, deferred, or declined

Done has evidence in a real domain event. Deferred stops blocking graduation but remains available. Declined is not offered again unless chosen in Settings.

Silence leaves an invitation pending but never marks it failed. Actions may satisfy commitments out of order.

The companion and Vel

The companion seat isn't owned by one character — any of the cast can sit with you, and the choice is yours. What this design does not decide is who greets a brand-new user. Day one calls it simply your companion. Vel is named when the person opens Rooms.

08 · Days two–five

Learn through invitations.

No hidden intake checklist appears. Willowden offers useful moments and lets understanding accumulate only from what the person does or chooses to share.

morning

One prepared block

Start it, make it smaller, choose something else, or say not today. If the app was closed, prepare it on next launch.

during the day

One good question

Vel may ask about one missing area at most once. Conversation never gates focus or graduation.

evening · optional

A soft settle

Time banked, one authored line, and tomorrow's tentative seed. No deficits or should-have list.

10 · Presence & consent

Warmth is not permission.

Willowden can become quieter as rhythm forms. That taper protects against permanent intensity; actual consent comes from separate, reversible choices.

Launch at loginYour companion and clock are ready without opening Home.Without it: start Willowden manually.
Native notificationsMorning invitations can arrive while Home is closed.Without it: wait until next launch.
Desktop activity sensingYour companion can notice idle or drift during a live block.Without it: the timer still works.
Away-from-desk tetherA chosen messaging service may carry invitations.Without it: remain desktop-only.
Morning/evening contactWillowden may initiate those moments.Without it: show them only when requested.

Ask contextually, at most one new permission in a session, only after the first payoff. A “no” stays no until the person changes it in Settings.

11 · Collections

Remember process without grading it.

Collection items mark meaningful firsts and returns. The day track fills inside a cycle and resets at its close. Neither becomes a ladder to protect.

First blockTime banked, however small.
Honest deferNot today was the right answer.
First cycleA plan chosen and frozen.
Welcome backReturned after time away.

One hero per moment

If a block resolves a commitment, fills the day track, and earns an item, only the collection item celebrates. Everything else updates quietly.

No overall rank

Growth reads starting, banking, and returning—never execution versus plan. Nothing shrinks during a cycle, and the final shape remains with its history.

12 · Journey states

Onboarding is not a pointer.

Account onboarding, device education, feature tours, re-entry, and replay are separate runs. Progress projects from real domain facts.

The journey keeps no second mutable copy of task, focus, helper, or cycle state. Each run pins a template version; active runs change only through an explicit migration.

13 · System shape

One native app. No local sidecar.

The background-capable Tauri process owns everything latency-, privacy-, or offline-critical. The service owns shared account and workspace truth.

14 · Failure & recovery

A product state, not a chain of screens.

The journey survives ordinary desktop life: dropped networks, sleep, updates, closed windows, and more than one device.

The computer sleepsSleep time is never silently counted as focus.

Another device moves aheadFacts reconcile without replaying celebrations.
15 · Requirements

Accessible and legible by default.

The first run asks for real tasks and observes behavior. Both the interface and its privacy boundary must be understandable.

Accessibility

  • Keyboard access to every beat and block action.
  • Predictable focus and persistent pause/skip controls.
  • Screen-reader equivalents for map, day track, color, and animation.
  • Reduced-motion alternatives for step-out, pulsing, and confetti.
  • Resizable or hidden companion window; always-on-top can be disabled.
  • Authored lines never vanish before they can be read.

Privacy

  • Raw app identifiers, idle readings, and window state stay local.
  • Synced facts remain visible: tasks, blocks, decisions, cycles, and Rooms.
  • The person can inspect evidence used for cycle one.
  • Collectors can be disabled without disabling focus blocks.
  • Devices, preferences, and stored data can be reviewed or removed.
  • No weather, precise location, titles, or content for novelty rewards.
16 · Measures

Useful action with low confusion.

Raw engagement time is not success. Deferral is not funnel failure. Control and recovery are measurable outcomes.

Primary

  • Signup → block start
  • First block started within five minutes
  • First block banked within fifteen
  • Return during days two–five
  • Cycle-one review reached

Diagnostic

  • Deferral by beat
  • Duration selected
  • Early bank vs planned completion
  • Fallback proposal rate
  • Companion placement choice

Guardrails

  • Never reward more notifications
  • Never punish less data sharing
  • Include accessibility paths
  • Analytics follow deletion rules
  • No optimization for fewer deferrals
17 · Delivery

Build the vertical experience first.

The first milestone is signup through one banked block—not a disconnected layer of infrastructure or UI.

Native foundation

Rust core, local database, durable outbox, focus recovery, registration, credential storage, and device approval.

Day-one slice

Signup → real thing → block → durable resume → one collection item. Include both companion placements and accessibility paths.

Settling-in days

Morning invitation, optional Vel questions, contextual permissions, visible learned-context review, and deterministic backoff.

Graduation

Ready-to-close state, mini-retro, bounded proposal, fallback, editing, scheduling, upcoming, and resting states.

Collections

General award rules, collection history, user-editable milestone stories, day-track fill, reset, and cycle record.
